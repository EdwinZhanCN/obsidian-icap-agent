A check reads a paragraph that you wrote, and asks about the points that do not hold. Your words stay in the note. You decide if you change them.

<AskDemo client:only="svelte" lang="en" kind="check" />

## Start a check

1. Select the passage, or put the cursor in the paragraph.
2. Open the command palette and run **Check what I wrote (selection or paragraph)**.

A check block shows below the paragraph. The agent reads your words, then asks a question.

## Answer the questions

1. Write your answer, then submit it.
2. Answer each follow-up question.

When the agent has no more questions, it writes a summary of what holds and what to change.

## Use the feedback

Select one of these:

- **revise my words**: write a new version of your paragraph. The plugin replaces your paragraph in the note with it, and keeps the feedback below.
- **keep feedback**: keep your paragraph as it is, with the feedback below it.

The feedback is a `feedback` callout. Its title has the date and the result.
